<strong>x2vx</strong> converts X (x.com), TikTok, Bluesky and Reddit links to embed-friendly proxy links and copies them to your clipboard, with tracking parameters removed.

<strong><font color="#339933">[Features]</font></strong>

* Converts links shared from any app via the Android share sheet.
* Removes query parameters such as <code>?s=46</code> and <code>?utm_source=...</code>.
* Choose the proxy for x.com links: vxtwitter.com, fixvx.com, fxtwitter.com or fixupx.com.
* Translate posts with fxtwitter.com / fixupx.com by picking a target language.
* TikTok links are converted to tnktok.com, Bluesky to fxbsky.app and Reddit to vxreddit.com.
* Optionally open the share menu after converting, to send the link straight to another app.
* Lightweight: no background processes, no network access.

<strong><font color="#339933">[How to use]</font></strong>

1. Tap Share on a post in X, TikTok, Bluesky or Reddit.
2. Choose x2vx.
3. The converted link is copied to your clipboard. Paste it anywhere.

Open x2vx from the app drawer to change the settings.

<strong><font color="#339933">[Note]</font></strong>

* x2vx doesn't watch the clipboard: Android doesn't allow apps to read the clipboard in the background, or to change links shared directly to other apps. Always share to x2vx first.
